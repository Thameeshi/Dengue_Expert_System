# Sources and Rule Traceability

## Knowledge Sources

### Primary Source

**Sri Lanka National Dengue Control Unit**
*"National Guidelines on Management of Dengue Fever & Dengue Haemorrhagic Fever in Adults – 2024"*

This is the primary clinical reference used for deriving the facts and rules implemented in this Expert System. The guideline provides comprehensive clinical management protocols for dengue fever in the Sri Lankan context, aligned with WHO recommendations.

### Supporting Sources

1. **World Health Organization (WHO)**
   *Dengue: Guidelines for Diagnosis, Treatment, Prevention and Control*
   WHO classification of dengue (Probable Dengue, Dengue with Warning Signs, Severe Dengue).

2. **World Health Organization (WHO)**
   *"Dengue and severe dengue"* — WHO Fact Sheet
   Overview of dengue clinical presentation, warning signs, and severe dengue criteria.

## Important Notes on Source Verification

- All facts and rules in this system are derived from the above published clinical guidelines.
- No rules were invented specifically for this implementation.
- No rules were generated by an AI/LLM for clinical content.
- Where exact page numbers from the primary source PDF have not been individually verified, this is noted in the traceability table below.
- The implementation team did not consult individual medical experts — all clinical knowledge is from published guidelines.
